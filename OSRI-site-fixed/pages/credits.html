What we will and will not publish

OSRI uses only images that are in the public domain, released under an open licence, or created by us. We do not use images found through a general image search without checking their licence, and we do not rely on a source being "probably fine".

The same standard applies to figures inside submitted manuscripts. If your paper reproduces a figure from a published source, you must have permission or the figure must be openly licensed, and the caption must credit it. A figure you drew yourself from data needs no permission — just cite the data.
