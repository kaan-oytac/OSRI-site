For authors

Submit a manuscript

Submissions are accepted year-round from students enrolled at a secondary school in the Okanagan Valley. There is no fee. First decisions are sent within four weeks of a complete submission.

Author

Name, school and grade for each. Each co-author must have contributed materially.

Manuscript

Ideally a shared Google Doc with comment access, or alternatively a PDF in a shared folder. Remove your name from the file itself.

250 words or fewer. For a mini-paper, one or two sentences.

Ethics

Who took part, how they were recruited, what they were asked, what was recorded, whether anything could identify them, how consent was obtained, and whether the school gave permission. The Ethics Review Panel assigns the tier from a corroboration of this description and the methodology section; see the ethics standard. You may respond with bullet points.

Name, role and email. Required for yellow-tier studies; the supervisor will be asked to co-sign.

AI disclosure

Name each tool used, what it was used for, and how you verified its output. Write "None" if no AI tools were used. See the policy.

Declaration

We use your email only to reply about this submission. It is never published. See the privacy notice.

Before you submit

  • Within the word limit for the format
  • APA 7 references; no name in the file
  • Sample sizes reported with every percentage
  • AI disclosure statement written
  • School permission obtained, if data was collected at school

What happens next

You will receive an acknowledgement within one week. If anything is missing, the editor will tell you exactly what. A first decision follows within four weeks of a complete submission.

Not sure which tier your study is?

Describe the design in the ethics section as fully as you can. The panel assigns the tier; you do not need to decide. If you are unsure whether a topic is red-tier, ask before collecting any data.
