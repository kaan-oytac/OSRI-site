Question bank

Research questions

Every question below is matched with at least one dataset or source that a student can actually obtain, a suggested method, and the ethics tier it would fall under. Questions are starting points: narrow them, combine them, or propose your own.

Wildfire, air & climate

How many instructional days in the Central Okanagan fell under a wildfire smoke advisory each year since 2017, and did attendance change on those days?

The valley has had a major smoke season most years since 2017. Nobody has published a school-level count.

Source
B.C. Ministry of Environment air quality archive (hourly PM2.5, Kelowna College station); Central Okanagan Public Schools attendance summaries, on request
Method
Count days over the 24-hour PM2.5 objective; compare attendance to the same weekday in non-smoke weeks
Ethics tier
Green (aggregate public data)

Has the first frost date in Kelowna, Vernon and Penticton moved later over the past 50 years, and by how much?

A clean, answerable climate question with three long station records to compare.

Dataset
Environment and Climate Change Canada historical climate data (daily minimum temperature, all three stations)
Method
Extract first date below 0 °C each autumn; linear trend with a confidence interval
Ethics tier
Green

How has the area burned within 20 km of Kelowna changed by decade, and does the 2003-2023 pattern differ from 1950-2000?

The historical fire perimeter layer goes back decades and is free to download.

Dataset
BC Wildfire Service historical fire perimeters (B.C. Data Catalogue)
Method
GIS buffer and area sum by decade (QGIS is free)
Ethics tier
Green

Water, lake & land

How closely do Okanagan Lake summer levels track the previous winter's snowpack, and has that relationship weakened?

Lake level is managed, so the answer is less obvious than it sounds.

Dataset
Water Survey of Canada lake level record for Okanagan Lake at Kelowna; B.C. River Forecast Centre snow survey bulletins (Mission Creek and Brenda Mines stations)
Method
Correlation of 1 April snow water equivalent with peak lake level, by decade
Ethics tier
Green

How many watercraft were intercepted carrying invasive mussels at B.C. inspection stations each year, and where were they coming from?

Zebra and quagga mussels would permanently change the lake. The inspection program publishes its numbers.

Dataset
B.C. Invasive Mussel Defence Program annual reports; Okanagan Basin Water Board Don't Move a Mussel materials
Method
Descriptive time series; origin-province breakdown
Ethics tier
Green

What share of the Okanagan's agricultural land has moved from orchards to vineyards since 1990, and where?

A land-use story visible from any highway and quantifiable from public agriculture data.

Dataset
Statistics Canada Census of Agriculture (Okanagan-Similkameen and Central Okanagan regional districts); B.C. Ministry of Agriculture tree fruit and grape acreage reports
Method
Acreage comparison by census year; map by regional district
Ethics tier
Green

Housing, community & economy

Is the 20-29 age cohort in the Kelowna metropolitan area shrinking faster than the provincial average, and does that track rental vacancy?

A question that Okanagan graduates feel personally and that public data can actually answer.

Dataset
Statistics Canada Census Profile (2016, 2021) for the Kelowna CMA; CMHC Rental Market Survey vacancy and average rent
Method
Cohort comparison across census years; plot against vacancy rate
Ethics tier
Green

How seasonal is employment in the Okanagan compared with the rest of B.C., and is the summer peak getting sharper?

Tourism and agriculture make the valley's labour market unusually seasonal.

Dataset
Statistics Canada Labour Force Survey, Thompson-Okanagan economic region, monthly
Method
Seasonal amplitude by year; compare with provincial series
Ethics tier
Green

How did the ordered evacuation population grow hour by hour during the August 2023 McDougall Creek fire?

Emergency orders are public records with timestamps. Reconstructing the timeline is real, publishable work.

Dataset
Central Okanagan Emergency Operations evacuation orders and alerts (archived bulletins); Statistics Canada dissemination area populations
Method
Join order polygons to dissemination area populations; cumulative timeline
Ethics tier
Green

School, behaviour & daily life

Questions in this group involve human participants. Only designs that meet the green tier are listed here: anonymous, non-sensitive, and collected with informed consent. Mental health, substance use and other red-tier topics are not accepted.

How does self-reported study location (home, library, school, café) relate to self-reported weekly study hours among senior students?

Simple, anonymous, and genuinely useful to a school's library.

Source
Anonymous survey designed by the student; OSRI can supply a template consent statement
Method
Cross-tabulation; report counts, not just percentages
Ethics tier
Green (anonymous, non-sensitive)

How full are the buses on the main school-serving routes at 8 a.m., and does it change between September and January?

Observational counts in a public setting: no participants, no consent burden.

Source
Direct passenger counts at a stop; BC Transit route schedules for the Kelowna Regional or Vernon Regional system
Method
Repeated counts on matched weekdays; report the counting protocol in full
Ethics tier
Green (public observation)

How much post-consumer food waste does one school cafeteria produce in a week, and which items dominate?

A weighing scale and permission from the cafeteria manager are all this needs.

Dataset
Direct measurement; comparison figures from Metro Vancouver and City of Kelowna waste composition studies
Method
Daily weighed counts; category breakdown
Ethics tier
Green

Research involving Indigenous communities

Questions about the Syilx Okanagan Nation, its lands, language or members are not listed here. Such research requires engagement with the Nation before it begins; see the ethics standard.

Suggest a research question

Students can add to this bank. Send a question you think is worth answering and, if it is accepted, it is published here credited to you by name and school. You do not have to answer it yourself.

One requirement: name a dataset or source where the data could actually be found. A question nobody can get data for is not yet a research question, and finding the source is most of the skill.

Write it as a question, specific enough that an answer would be a number, a trend or a comparison. "Is the lake getting warmer?" is a topic; "How has August surface temperature in Okanagan Lake changed from 1990 to 2026?" is a question.

Where would the data come from? A link, a report, an agency, or "I would collect it by…". Required for questions that need data analysis by the student to obtain a conclusion.

Two or three sentences. What would we know that we do not know now?

We use your email only to reply about this submission. It is never published. See the privacy notice.

Okanagan Student Research Initiative

A student-run venue for secondary school research in the Okanagan Valley. OSRI suggests research questions matched to real datasets, reviews manuscripts against a published ethics standard, and hosts the finished work online at no cost to authors.

OSRI ethics review is not IRB or REB approval and does not substitute for it.
